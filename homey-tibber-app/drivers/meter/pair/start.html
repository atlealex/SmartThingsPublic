This app tracks this month's electricity cost and gives a full-month estimate, using your Tibber Pulse consumption data.

  1. Log in at developer.tibber.com with your Tibber account and generate a personal access token.
  2. Decide whether you pay spot price or a fixed price for electricity, and whether your supplier charges a markup or a fixed monthly fee - you'll enter these on the next screen.
  3. If you also have the Elvia-Nett app installed and paired, grid rent (nettleie) is included automatically.
