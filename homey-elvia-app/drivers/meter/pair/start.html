This app reads grid tariff prices (and, optionally, your personal hourly consumption) from Elvia's API.

  1. Sign up at elvia.portal.azure-api.net and subscribe to the "Grid tariff" product to get a subscription key.
  2. Find your metering point ID (målepunkt-ID) in Elvia's customer portal or on your meter.
  3. Optional: to also read personal hourly consumption, log in at elvid.no and copy the access token it gives you (it expires after a while and must be refreshed manually in the device settings).
